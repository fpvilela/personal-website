# Confirmed Decisions

## Site Direction

- The site will follow an interactive resume direction.
- The site will not follow a traditional commercial portfolio direction.
- `Home` will be the main page of the experience.

## Site Pages

- The site will have three pages:
  - `Home`
  - `Projects`
  - `Resume`

## Navigation

- The site will have a `Navbar` as its main navigation element.
- The `Navbar` will include the main anchors:
  - `Home`
  - `Projects`
  - `Resume`
  - `Contact`
- `Home` will have an auxiliary vertical navigation bar with all sections present in the `Home` page.
- The auxiliary vertical navigation bar will exist only in `Home`.

## Hero

- `Home` will have a `Hero`.
- The `Hero` will have three CTAs:
  - `View Projects`
  - `View Resume`
  - A CTA to talk to AI
- The AI conversation CTA will be aimed at a resume assistant.
- The resume assistant will not be designed as a generic chatbot.

## About Me

- `Home` will have an `About Me` section.
- The `About Me` section will be placed right after the `Hero`.

## Experiences

- `Home` will have an `Experiences` section.
- The `Experiences` section will be placed right after `About Me`.
- The `Experiences` section will be the main core of the professional journey reading flow.
- The `Experiences` section will be structured as a vertical timeline.
- Each `Experiences` item will be named after the experience title itself, based on `resume/experiences.yaml`.
- Each `Experiences` item will include company, roles, and projects as related elements.

## Roles

- `Home` will have a `Roles` section.
- The `Roles` section will exist as a dedicated layer for explaining the roles linked from `Experiences`.
- Role descriptions will be centralized in `Roles` instead of being fully expanded inside each `Experiences` item.

## Companies

- `Home` will have a `Companies` section.
- The `Companies` section will exist as a dedicated layer for the companies linked from `Experiences`.
- Companies will remain related elements inside `Experiences` and will also have a dedicated area in `Home`.

## Projects

- `Home` will have a `Projects` section.
- The `Projects` section in `Home` will show only selected projects.
- The `Projects` page will contain the full project list.
- Skills attached to projects will be used as informational labels or filters.

## Skills

- `Home` will have a `Skills` section.
- Each skill will display its corresponding competency level.
- The `Skills` section will include a legend explaining what each competency level represents.
- Full competency descriptions will not be repeated for every individual skill.

## Education

- `Home` will have an `Education` section.
- The `Education` section will be a main section of `Home`.

## Courses

- `Courses` will not be a main standalone section of `Home`.
- `Courses` will be presented as a complementary block associated with `Education`.

## Contact

- `Home` will have a `Contact` section as its final section before the footer.
- The `Contact` section will function as the closing section of the `Home` page.
- The `Contact` section will include a short introduction.
- The `Contact` section will be organized into two internal groups:
  - direct contact
  - professional presence
- The direct contact group will include:
  - `Email`
  - `Phone`
- The professional presence group will include:
  - `LinkedIn`
  - `GitHub`
  - `YouTube`
